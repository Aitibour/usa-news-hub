About AmericaPulse.live

Independent American news since 2000

Our Story

AmericaPulse.live was founded in 2000 with a single mission: deliver fast, accurate, and unbiased news to the American public. Born in the early days of online journalism, we have covered every major chapter of modern American life — from the 2000 presidential election to September 11, two decades of foreign policy, financial crises, cultural shifts, and the technology revolution that reshaped how the country communicates.

Over more than two decades, AmericaPulse has grown from a small editorial team into a full-spectrum digital newsroom covering politics, business, technology, sports, health, world affairs, and entertainment. Our commitment has never changed: report the facts, hold power accountable, and give every reader the context they need to form their own view.

Today, AmericaPulse.live reaches readers across all 50 states and beyond, delivering breaking news around the clock at americapulse.live.

Editorial Standards

Every story published on AmericaPulse.live is held to strict accuracy and fairness standards. We correct errors promptly, disclose conflicts of interest, and never accept payment for editorial coverage. Our newsroom operates independently of advertisers and outside investors.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate the site:

  • Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage data (pages visited, time on site) to understand how readers use the site. This data is aggregated and not linked to individuals.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: If you subscribe, we collect your email address to send you news updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Contact forms: If you contact us, we collect your name, email, and message to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences (e.g., cookie consent choice)

You can decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown on your first visit. Declining cookies will not affect your ability to read articles.
